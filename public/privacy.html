BandForge

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

BandForge is a music studio that runs entirely in your web browser. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it, including when you publish a song to YouTube.

Your songs and settings

Your songs, recordings, album covers and settings are saved in your own browser's storage on your device. They are not uploaded to any BandForge server. BandForge does not have accounts and does not run a server that stores your data. If you clear your browser data, they are deleted; use Export Project File to keep a backup.

Jam Sessions

When you start or join a Jam Session, the song you share is sent directly between the browsers in the session (peer to peer). Public signalling servers are used only to help the browsers find each other.

Publishing to YouTube

BandForge uses YouTube API Services. If you choose Publish to YouTube, you sign in with Google and grant BandForge permission to:

  • Upload videos to your YouTube channel (youtube.upload): to upload the video you create and set its thumbnail.
  • See your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to show which channel you're publishing to and list your playlists.
  • Manage your YouTube account (youtube.force-ssl): only requested if you choose a playlist, to add the new video to it.

BandForge only uses this access when you press Publish, and only to upload the song you chose, with the title, description, tags, thumbnail and settings you see in the dialog. It never deletes or changes your other videos.

The sign-in token Google gives BandForge stays in your browser's memory for that browser tab and expires within an hour. It is never sent to or stored on any BandForge server. Your channel name, picture and playlist names are only displayed to you and are not stored or shared. The ID of the video you published is saved with your song in your browser so BandForge can link to it.

BandForge's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. BandForge does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or let anyone read it.

By publishing, you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove BandForge's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or with Sign out in the Publish to YouTube dialog.

Things BandForge loads from other sites

To play instruments, BandForge downloads sound samples from public sites (such as GitHub Pages). Those sites can see your IP address, as with any web request. BandForge does not use analytics, tracking cookies or ads.

Children

BandForge does not knowingly collect information from anyone. YouTube publishing requires a Google account that meets Google's age requirements.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions or requests: open an issue on BandForge's GitHub page.
